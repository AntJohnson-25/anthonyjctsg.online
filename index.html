    Categories FAQ

    Free browser games by category

    Every game here is free and plays right in your browser on a computer, phone or tablet: no download, no account. Pick a category to find your kind of game.

    Card games

    Classic card games against computer opponents.

    • Spades Partnership Spades in 3D at a seaside villa. Pick your player, walk the terrace and sit at one of four tables, from Rookie to Shark, with your partner across from you. First team to 500 wins.

    Action and fighting games

    Fast fights, arena brawls and a zombie last stand.

    • Bladed A knives-only 3D arena fight on a superyacht. Stab, lunge and throw through Team Deathmatch or Free-for-all against bots, and chain 5 kills to call a Rogue Wave.
    • Ant's Combat Gauntlet A Wild West fighting game. Brawl past six goons to qualify, then fight through a gauntlet of seven rivals with fists, flips and dual revolvers.
    • Zombie Wave A pixel-art side-scrolling shooter. Hold the alley against 15 waves of walkers, runners and crawlers before the horde breaks through.
    • My Block A third-person street war. Pack silver-wrapped bricks into duffle bags at abandoned houses, carry them home to your block, and fight off rivals with guns, fists, kicks and a flying shoulder throw.
    • Snowball Squabble A third-person team snowball fight. Pick one of 30 fighters, hide behind snow mounds and ice castles, wind up and throw, dive-roll under incoming snowballs, and be the last team standing, or play capture the flag.

    Shooter games

    Free shooting games: gun fights on the street and a zombie last stand.

    • My Block A third-person shooter set on a gloomy street block. Take cover behind highway barriers, shoot it out with rival crews, and carry the duffle bags home.
    • Zombie Wave A pixel-art side-scrolling zombie shooter. Hold the alley against 15 waves of walkers, runners and crawlers before the horde breaks through.

    Racing and running games

    Run, jump and race to the finish.

    • Wealth Runner 2 A run for the money. Keep moving, keep stacking, and see how far your decisions carry you before the run ends.
    • X-Parkour A three-way extreme parkour race with street fighting: wall-run, climb, swing over gaps and punch your two rivals on the way to the finish. Best of 3.

    Sports games

    Pick up a paddle or a driver.

    • King of the Dill Third-person 3D pickleball. Swipe to swing, time your release, and win an 8-player tournament or a quick match against the computer.
    • Fairway Kings 3D golf. Nine holes at Kingsway National with an analog swing, spin, wind and real putting breaks. Play stroke play, take on your rival, or go head to head on one screen.
    • Dodge It 3D team dodgeball. Pick an athlete, lead your team from 1v1 to 5v5, and throw, catch, block and flip out of the way. A catch puts the thrower out and brings a teammate back. Plays with keyboard and mouse, or on a phone with two thumbs.
    • Cornhole 3D backyard cornhole under the floodlights. Pick a player from the crew, aim, draw back and toss for the hole in 1v1 or 2v2, with cancellation scoring. Plays with the mouse, or with one slide of the thumb on a phone.
    • Going, Going, Gone 3D home run derby. Pick a slugger, time one button against the pitching machine, and watch every hit measured in feet, exit speed and launch angle. Fifteen pitches a bucket, presented by ctsg-htx.com.

    Business and tycoon games

    Start small, make smart money moves, and build an empire.

    • Billionaire Barber 2 Cut hair. Build wealth. Own the city. Choose your barber and work your way up from a single chair.
    • buy'n'scale Acquire, improve, compound in 3D. Start with $150,000, walk up to real businesses, buy them, fix what's broken, and let the multiple do the rest.
    • Wealth Runner 2 The money game on the run: every choice you make on the road decides how wealthy you finish.
    • Food Truck Billionaire From a hot dog cart to a billion, in 3D. Work the cart, buy your first truck, cook the line yourself and build a fleet.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are the games on AnthonyJCTSG Games free?

    Yes. Every game is free to play in your web browser. There is nothing to buy and no account to make.

    Do I need to download or install anything?

    No. Every game runs right in the browser (HTML5 and WebGL). Open the page and press Play.

    Can I play these games on my phone?

    Yes. The games work on phones and tablets with touch controls, as well as on desktop with a keyboard and mouse. The 3D games play best with the phone turned sideways.

    Where can I play Spades online for free?

    Right here: Spades is free in your browser. It is classic partnership Spades in 3D at a seaside villa: pick your player, sit at one of four tables (Rookie, Regular, Sharp or Shark) and play with your computer partner against two computer opponents. First team to 500 wins.

    What kinds of games are there?

    Card games (Spades), action and fighting games (Snowball Squabble, My Block, Bladed, Ant's Combat Gauntlet, Zombie Wave), shooter games (My Block, Zombie Wave),racing and running games (Wealth Runner 2, X-Parkour), sports games (Going, Going, Gone, a 3D home run derby; Fairway Kings, 3D golf; Dodge It, 3D dodgeball; Cornhole, 3D cornhole; King of the Dill, 3D pickleball) and business tycoon games (Billionaire Barber 2, Food Truck Billionaire, buy'n'scale).

    Are there free shooting games I can play in my browser?

    Yes. My Block is a free third-person shooter where you lead a crew through a gloomy street block, take cover behind barriers and fight rival crews. Zombie Wave is a free pixel-art zombie shooter where you hold an alley against 15 waves of the undead. Both run in your browser with no download, on desktop or phone.

    Is there a free snowball fight game I can play online?

    Yes. Snowball Squabble is a free third-person team snowball fight that runs in your browser. Choose one of 30 fighters, wear a team-colour ski mask, hide behind snow mounds and ice castles, hold to wind up a throw and let go, dive-roll under incoming snowballs and pack more snow. In elimination one hit and you're out, and the last team standing wins the round; in capture the flag you steal the other team's flag and carry it back to your castle. It plays on desktop with a keyboard and mouse, or on a phone with touch controls (turn it sideways), with no download and no account.

    Who makes these games?

    Anthony C Johnson, an independent game developer in Houston, Texas. More about him is at anthonyjctsg.com.
