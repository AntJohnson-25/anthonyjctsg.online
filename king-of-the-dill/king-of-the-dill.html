How to play

  • You don't run, you swing. Your player moves to the ball on their own.
  • Swipe up to hit. Angle = aim. A faster swipe hits harder; a short, slow one drops it soft.
  • Tap for a dink. Swipe down for a lob. High ball + hard swipe = smash.
  • Timing: let go as the ring closes on the ball. Green = PERFECT.
  • A red marker means the ball is going out: don't swing, let it bounce.
  • Rules: serve crosscourt. The serve and the return must bounce. No volleys from the kitchen (the zone by the net). Only the server scores.
  • Keyboard: Space/X drive, Z dink, C lob, arrows aim and depth, Shift = power, N net/back, P pause.
