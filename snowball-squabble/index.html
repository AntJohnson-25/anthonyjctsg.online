Snowball Squabble

Third-person snow warfare  ·  two teams, one hit and you're out  ·  or steal the CTSG flag

Settings & controls
Phone: play in landscape. Left thumb moves, drag the right side to look. Hold THROW (slide to aim) and let go to throw.
  • W A S D move   Shift sprint   C crouch
  • Mouse aim   hold Left click to wind up, release to throw
  • R hold to pack more snowballs   E build a mound (4 snowballs)
  • Q / Space dive roll: low enough to duck under throws
  • Capture the flag: run into the enemy's CTSG flag to grab it, bring it back to your own flag's stand. Your flag has to be home to score. Hit players respawn after 8 s; the carrier survives two hits.
